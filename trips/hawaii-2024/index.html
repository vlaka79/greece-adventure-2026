The story

Our honeymoon — Kauai, the Big Island, and a Waikiki finish.

Island-hopping through September: Kauai for mountain tubing, then the Big Island for abalone and several nights at Hilton Waikoloa.

Late September into early October at the Royal Hawaiian on Waikiki — Pearl Harbor and the USS Arizona, Maui Brewing in the evening, and an easy Avis car for getting around town.

Home by October 2. The map below is the islands only.
