The story

One trip. Miami first, then two ships.

Flew out of Burbank, through Denver, into Miami. A few nights at AC Hotel Brickell. Dinner at Crazy About You on the bay. A Big Bus ticket for the city, and a Key West day trip before we boarded.

Virgin Voyages Brilliant Lady, Miami to Miami, eleven nights on Southern Caribbean and Aruban Nights. Aruba we rented a car and drove the island. Cartagena overnight — a city tour on the 6th. A Shore Thing in Ocho Rios — river tubing, rafting, and Bamboo Beach. Grand Cayman for a snorkeling excursion. The meals that stuck: The Test Kitchen and Pink Agave.

Overnight in Riviera Beach, then Margaritaville at Sea Paradise out of Port of Palm Beach for a three-night Nassau escape. In Nassau we did a city-tour excursion. Home to LAX after that.
