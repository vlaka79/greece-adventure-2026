The story

Italy with Julia — Rome first, then Matera, Maiori, Pompeii, and Caserta.

Mid-February in Rome at NH Collection Roma Palazzo Cinquecento, with a Colosseum arena-floor morning before the train south.

Late March brought Matera and Palazzo del Duca. Early April was Maiori — Airbnb Costa Major for two nights and a TRAVELMAR ferry along the coast.

Caserta in mid-April at Hotel Royal Caserta, a Pompeii morning with an archaeologist on the 12th, then the train out the next morning.
