  1. August 19, 2026Pre-trip

    The plan, as booked

    Five days out. We leave from Burbank on the 24th and land in Chania late on the 25th. Crete first — Chania, Paleochora, Douliana, Elounda, and Heraklion — before a morning ferry to Santorini on the 9th and on to Athens on the 12th. Home on the 17th. We’ll fill this in from the road.

    Rough itinerary

    A sketch of the days — not a locked schedule.

    1. August 24–25

      Travel

      Burbank → Chania

      Heading out Monday afternoon from Burbank. We land in Chania late Tuesday night.

    2. August 25–30

      Crete

      Chania, Crete

      Slow days by the harbour, and the Saturday market — Mercato del Sabato.

    3. August 30 – September 1

      Crete

      Paleochora

      Down to the south coast. We pick up a car in Chania that morning, and plan to visit Elafonissi Beach.

    4. September 1–4

      Crete

      Douliana

      Three nights in a mountain village inland from the north coast.

    5. September 4–6

      Crete

      Elounda

      Two nights on the northeast coast, near Agios Nikolaos.

    6. September 6–9

      Crete

      Heraklion

      Last stretch on Crete. We drop the car here on the 6th. One stop that matters to us: Saint Minas Cathedral, a family site.

    7. September 9

      Santorini

      Heraklion → Santorini

      Morning Seajets ferry, 8:00–9:35.

    8. September 9–12

      Santorini

      Santorini

      Three nights on the island.

    9. September 12

      Athens

      Santorini → Athens

      Afternoon Seajets ferry to Piraeus, 12:55–18:30.

    10. September 12–17

      Athens

      Athens

      Last days in the city. The Parthenon, a few museums, and hunting for hidden bars in the Plaka. Homeward on the 17th.

    11. September 17

      Travel

      Athens → Burbank

      Early start from Athens. Back in Burbank that evening.
