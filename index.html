Slow days by the harbour, and the Saturday market — Mercato del Sabato.

Crete

Down to the south coast. We pick up a car in Chania that morning, and plan to visit Elafonissi Beach.

September 1–4
Crete

Douliana

Three nights in a mountain village inland from the north coast.

September 4–6
Crete

Elounda

Two nights on the northeast coast, near Agios Nikolaos.

September 6–9
Crete

Heraklion

Last stretch on Crete. We drop the car here on the 6th. One stop that matters to us: Saint Minas Cathedral, a family site.
